## Overview

When JusticeCompass doesn't have enough information in its knowledge base to answer a question with confidence, it should:

1. **State clearly**: "Based on available case law in the JusticeCompass database, we have insufficient precedents for this specific scenario."
2. **Explain why**: What type of information is missing
3. **Provide statutory info**: Even without cases, explain what the law says
4. **Recommend resources**: Direct to reliable sources
5. **Suggest action**: Recommend next steps

## Reliable Indian Law Resources

### Tier 1: Government Official Sources (Most Authoritative)

#### Legislative Sources
- **Ministry of Law & Justice** (https://legislative.gov.in)
  - Acts and Bills of India
  - Latest amendments
  - Official text of all statutes
  - **Best for**: Finding current statute text, amendments

- **e-Governance Website** (https://www.eCourts.gov.in)
  - Case filing, status tracking
  - Court information
  - **Best for**: Finding court contacts, filing procedures

#### Supreme Court
- **Official Website** (https://main.sci.gov.in)
  - Judgments searchable by case number, year, judge
  - Case status and hearing dates
  - Court procedures and rules
  - **Best for**: Supreme Court precedents, high-profile cases

#### High Courts (State-wise)
Each state has an official High Court website:

| State | URL | Specialization |
|-------|-----|-----------------|
| Delhi | https://delhihighcourt.gov.in | National Capital |
| Bombay | https://www.bombayhighcourt.nic.in | Western India |
| Madras | https://www.madhighcourt.gov.in | South India |
| Calcutta | https://www.calcuttahighcourt.gov.in | Eastern India |
| Punjab | https://punjabhighcourt.gov.in | North India |
| Gujarat | https://gujarathighcourt.nic.in | Western India |

**Best for**: State-specific law, jurisdiction-specific rulings, local bench decisions

#### Legal Services Authority
- **Portal**: https://nls.org.in (National)
- **State Portals**: Each state has a legal services authority
  - **Delhi**: https://www.delhilegalservices.in
  - **Maharashtra**: https://maharashtralegalsservices.nic.in
  - **Tamil Nadu**: https://tnlsc.in
- **Best for**: Free legal aid, legal advice clinics, state-specific information

#### National Judicial Data Grid (NJDP)
- **Portal**: https://njdp.ecourts.gov.in
- **Data**: Case status from e-courts across India
- **Best for**: Checking pending case status, finding district courts

### Tier 2: Comprehensive Legal Databases (High Quality)

#### IndianKanoon.org
- **URL**: https://indiankanoon.org
- **Content**: 20+ million court judgments (1950-present)
- **Coverage**: All courts - Supreme, High, District, Tribunal
- **Search**: By case name, parties, judge, date range, keywords
- **Export**: Can export judgment texts
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Mobile**: Mobile-friendly interface
- **Best for**:
  - Finding cases similar to yours
  - Understanding how courts have decided similar cases
  - Researching judge's previous decisions
  - Tracking case trends

**How to search effectively**:
```
1. Go to https://indiankanoon.org
2. Search by:
   - Case type: "bail petition", "divorce petition", "tenancy dispute"
   - Section: "IPC 302" or "Article 21"
   - Judge name: Find how a judge decides similar cases
   - Keywords: "bail conditions", "property dispute", "maintenance"
3. Filter by:
   - Year range (recent = more applicable)
   - Court (High Court more authoritative than District)
4. Read 5-10 similar cases to understand patterns
```

#### Google Scholar India
- **URL**: https://scholar.google.com (click India filter)
- **Content**: Indian court judgments, articles, books
- **Coverage**: Supreme Court, High Courts primarily
- **Search**: Simple keyword search
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Advantage**: Better search interface than IndianKanoon
- **Best for**: Quick searches, recent important cases, High Court decisions

### Tier 3: Legal Research & Case Reporters (Professional)

#### All India Reporter (AIR)
- **Availability**: 
  - Free at law libraries
  - Partial free online via legal portals
  - Subscription: https://www.airindia.online
- **Content**: Reported cases (curated subset of important cases)
- **Quality**: High (professionally edited summaries)
- **Best for**: Landmark cases, well-reasoned judgments

#### SCC Online (Supreme Court Cases)
- **URL**: https://www.scconline.com
- **Content**: Supreme Court and High Court cases
- **Cost**: Subscription (₹3,000-10,000/month)
- **Quality**: Excellent (with summaries and headnotes)
- **Features**: Citation tracking, case analytics
- **Best for**: Professional legal research (requires subscription)

#### Manupatra
- **URL**: https://www.manupatra.com
- **Content**: All Indian courts, comprehensive
- **Cost**: Subscription
- **Quality**: Very high
- **Best for**: Serious legal research, professionals

### Tier 4: Specialized Resources by Area

#### Criminal Law

**IPC vs BNS Comparison**:
- **IndianKanoon**: Search "IPC to BNS mapping", "criminal law amendments"
- **Ministry of Law**: Official BNS 2023 text at legislative.gov.in
- **Bail Information**:
  - Search "bail conditions" on IndianKanoon
  - Check case type: "bail petition" + your case type

**Murder/Serious Crime**:
- **Landmark cases**: Search "State vs [name]" on IndianKanoon
- **Punishment research**: Look up IPC/BNS sections + "punishment" + case name
- **Bail precedents**: "Murder bail conditions" on IndianKanoon

#### Family Law

**Marriage/Divorce**:
- **Applicable acts**:
  - Hindu Marriage Act (1955)
  - Special Marriage Act (1954)
  - Personal law of your religion
- **Search**: "Divorce grounds", "alimony calculation", "custody" on IndianKanoon

**Maintenance/Alimony**:
- **Relevant acts**: CrPC Section 125, Family Law specific acts
- **Cases**: Search "maintenance calculation", "alimony quantum"
- **Legal Aid**: Contact state legal services for women's rights cells

#### Tenancy & Property

**Rent Control** (varies by state):
- **Delhi**: Delhi Rent Act
- **Maharashtra**: Maharashtra Rent Control Act
- **Tamil Nadu**: TNRRRLT Act 2017
- **Search**: State name + "rent control" + "eviction"
- **Resources**: State housing board websites

**Property Disputes**:
- **Basic law**: Transfer of Property Act, 1882
- **Cases**: Search "property dispute", "title", "adverse possession"

#### Consumer Protection

**Complaints**:
- **Applicable**: Consumer Protection Act 2019 (replaced 1986)
- **Authorities**: District Consumer Commission → State → National
- **Process**: File with District Consumer Dispute Redressal Commission
- **Help**: Contact state consumer protection authority

## By Case Type: Resource Recommendations

### Criminal Cases

**Immediate Actions**:
1. Read IPC/BNS sections on IndianKanoon
2. Search "[Section number] bail" + "High Court" for precedents
3. Contact:
   - State legal services (free legal aid)
   - Criminal lawyer
   - Bar council (lawyer referral)

**Key Resources**:
- IndianKanoon: Search by section + "bail" or "punishment"
- Google Scholar: Latest HC/SC judgments
- Legal Services: Free consultation

**Timeline**: Time-sensitive! File bail quickly.

### Family Law Cases (Marriage, Divorce, Custody)

**Immediate Actions**:
1. Identify applicable act (Hindu/Muslim/Christian/Special Marriage Act)
2. Understand key sections on IndianKanoon
3. Find precedents on maintenance/alimony/custody calculations
4. Consult family law specialist

**Key Resources**:
- IndianKanoon: Search case type + section
- Legal Services: Free helpline often available
- State High Court: Family law divisions

**Timeline**: Can take 1-3 years. Start early.

### Tenancy & Property Disputes

**Immediate Actions**:
1. Check applicable state law (varies!)
2. Understand tenant/landlord rights for your state
3. Research eviction procedures and timelines
4. Check if property law applies

**Key Resources**:
- IndianKanoon: "[Your state] rent control" + "eviction"
- State housing board
- Legal Services: Property dispute resolution

**Timeline**: Can take 2-5 years.

### Consumer Complaints

**Immediate Actions**:
1. Keep all documentation (purchase proof, communications, etc.)
2. Send complaint letter to seller/manufacturer (registered mail)
3. If no response: File with District Consumer Commission (free)
4. Fill Form 1 (available at commission website)

**Key Resources**:
- State Consumer Protection Authority website
- National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000 (toll-free)
- District Consumer Commission (search: district + "consumer commission")

**Timeline**: 3-6 months for disposal (fast compared to courts).

## Step-by-Step: Finding Information on Your Own

### Method 1: Using IndianKanoon

```
1. Go to https://indiankanoon.org
2. In search box, try:
   - Specific section: "IPC 302" (murder)
   - Case type: "bail petition"
   - Judge: "[Judge name]"
   - Keywords: "bail conditions", "domestic violence"
3. Use filters on left:
   - Court: Filter by High Court/Supreme Court (higher authority)
   - Year: Recent cases more relevant (2022 onwards)
4. Read judgment summaries
5. For full text: Click case name
6. Look for:
   - "HELD" or "ORDER" section (decision)
   - Key factors considered
   - Relevant sections cited
7. Print/save judgment
8. Discuss with lawyer
```

### Method 2: Using Google Scholar

```
1. Go to https://scholar.google.com
2. Click "Legal opinions and journals"
3. Filter: India only
4. Search:
   - Case type: "divorce petition", "theft prosecution"
   - Section number: "IPC 498A"
5. Review results (sort by relevance/date)
6. Click case name for full text
7. Look for citations to other important cases
8. Follow those citations for more context
```

### Method 3: Using Official Court Websites

```
1. Identify your court:
   - District level: e-courts.gov.in → select state → district
   - High Court: [state]highcourt.gov.in
2. Look for:
   - "Judgments" or "Case law"
   - "Rules and procedures"
   - "Filing procedures"
3. Contact registry:
   - Phone: Usually on website
   - Email: Inquiry email
   - In person: Court building
4. Ask:
   - "How do I file a [case type]?"
   - "What are the fees?"
   - "What documents are needed?"
   - "How long does it take?"
```

## Consulting a Lawyer: What to Do

### How to Find a Lawyer

1. **Legal Services Authority** (Free or low-cost)
   - Visit your district/state legal services office
   - **Qualification**: Low income or specific case types
   - **Cost**: Usually free
   - **Quality**: Qualified advocates, experienced

2. **Bar Council**
   - Every state/district has a bar council
   - Request: "Advocate referral" or "panel of advocates"
   - **Cost**: Usually ₹500-2,000 for initial consultation
   - **Find**: District bar association website

3. **Personal Recommendations**
   - Friends, family with experience
   - Employer legal helpline
   - Alumni networks

4. **Online Platforms**
   - IndianLawyers.net
   - LawRato.com
   - Advocate.com
   - **Caution**: Verify credentials, read reviews

### First Consultation Checklist

When meeting a lawyer, bring:
- [ ] All relevant documents (agreements, communications, etc.)
- [ ] Timeline of events
- [ ] List of witnesses
- [ ] Questions prepared in advance
- [ ] Note: Initial consultation fees vary (₹500-5,000)

**Questions to ask**:
1. "What are my legal options?"
2. "What's the likely timeline?"
3. "What will this cost?"
4. "What documents do I need?"
5. "What are the chances of success?"
6. "How often will we communicate?"
7. "Who will handle my case day-to-day?"

## Templates: What to Write When Resources Are Insufficient

### Template: Insufficient Data Response

```
INSUFFICIENT DATA MESSAGE:

I have searched the JusticeCompass database for [case type / statute / 
situation]. While I found some relevant [law/cases], I don't have 
sufficient documented precedents for your specific situation to provide 
a high-confidence answer.

HERE'S WHAT I DID FIND:
- [Applicable statute: Section X says...]
- [General principle: Courts typically consider...]
- [Similar cases exist but with different facts]

HERE'S WHAT'S MISSING:
- [Specific type of case precedent needed]
- [Jurisdiction-specific law that varies by state]
- [Recent amendment that may apply]

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO:

1. RESEARCH:
   → Search IndianKanoon.org for "[search terms]"
   → Read similar cases to understand patterns
   → Time to spend: 30-60 minutes

2. LEGAL CONSULTATION:
   → Consult a [type] lawyer
   → Bring relevant documents
   → Ask about your specific facts
   → Time to book: 1-2 days
   → Cost: ₹500-2,000 typical

3. FREE RESOURCES:
   → Contact [State] Legal Services Authority
   → Phone: [local contact]
   → Website: [official portal]

RELIABLE SOURCES FOR THIS TOPIC:
- Government: [specific portal/section]
- Case law: [search string for IndianKanoon]
- Experts: [association/organization]

The more specific information you can provide, the better I can guide you.
```

### Template: Insufficient Data for Specific Issue

```
CASE OUTCOME PROBABILITY: NOT AVAILABLE YET

I don't have enough case data to reliably predict outcomes for [case type] 
in your jurisdiction.

WHAT I CAN TELL YOU:
- The law states: [statute section quote]
- Key factors courts consider: [list]
- Historical pattern: [if available - e.g., "60% of similar cases resulted in X"]

WHAT I CANNOT TELL YOU:
- Probability your specific case will succeed
- How your specific judge typically rules
- What verdict you'll receive

WHY?
- Your situation has unique facts
- Judicial outcomes are inherently uncertain
- My database may not have cases exactly like yours

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO:
- Discuss these factors with a lawyer
- Lawyer has experience with your judge
- Lawyer knows local court procedures
- Lawyer can assess your specific evidence

This is one of the limitations of AI-based legal analysis. Human judgment 
of an experienced lawyer is still essential for case-specific advice.
```
